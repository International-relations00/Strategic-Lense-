About Me

I am Zubair Akhtar Safi, an undergraduate student of International Relations at the University of Gujrat, Pakistan, with a strong interest in international politics, geopolitics, foreign policy, diplomacy, and global affairs.

My academic interests focus on understanding the changing international system, great-power competition, regional security, South Asian affairs, Pakistan's foreign policy, and the political dynamics that shape relations among states. I am particularly interested in examining international issues through analytical and evidence-based approaches.

Alongside my university studies, I am developing my skills in academic writing, research, political analysis, critical thinking, and policy-oriented research. Through this platform, I publish my articles, analytical writings, research work, academic essays, and reflections on contemporary international affairs.

I believe that studying International Relations is not simply about understanding what happens between states; it is also about examining the historical, political, economic, strategic, and social forces behind those developments.

This website serves as my personal academic and publication platform, where I document my intellectual journey, share my research and writing, and engage with important questions concerning international politics and global affairs.
